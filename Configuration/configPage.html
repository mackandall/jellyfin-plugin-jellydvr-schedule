Favourites

Record by title, whatever id the guide gives the show, so a guide change can't silently break it (a Jellyfin series recording is tied to the guide's series id). Each episode is recorded once, at its earliest matching airing. Exact title ignores case, punctuation, a leading "The" and a year in brackets; Keyword matches those words anywhere in the title. Save to apply; favourites are checked every few minutes and after each guide refresh.

Series priority

Highest first. When recordings clash, the lower one gives way: it moves to another airing of the same episode if one fits, otherwise it is skipped.

Settings

A copy already recording, or one you scheduled by hand, is always the one kept.

Live TV and recordings

When a recording is about to start and people watching live TV hold every tuner input. Viewers on the recording's own channel share its input and are never interrupted.
Who gives way: the channel with the fewest viewers, then the one tuned to most recently.

Recordings that don't start

Jellyfin opens live streams one at a time, so a tuner that connects but never sends anything holds up every recording and live channel, with nothing in the guide to show it.

Recordings that stall

A tuner can go quiet in the middle of a recording. Jellyfin doesn't notice: it keeps the recording open to the end and writes nothing more, so the file is short with nothing to show why.
The stalled recording is stopped and the rest of the programme is recorded as a second file. Off: only the alert.
